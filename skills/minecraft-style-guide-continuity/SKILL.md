# Minecraft Style Guide and Continuity

## Purpose
Preserve visual consistency across a growing pack, server, mod or asset library.

## Trigger
Use when creating multiple related assets, handing work between agents, or extending an established visual identity.

## Workflow
1. Collect approved reference assets and identify shared visual rules.
2. Record palette tokens, shape language, proportions, texture resolution, light direction, outline policy, material rules and detail density.
3. Add examples of approved and rejected patterns.
4. Define asset-class variants (mob, block, item, UI, environment) without breaking the shared identity.
5. Version the guide and note why each rule changed.
6. Compare new assets against a contact sheet of approved assets.
7. Record intentional exceptions and who approved them.

## Outputs
Versioned style guide, asset reference board, palette/material tokens, approved examples and change log.

## Safety
Do not absorb unlicensed assets into the reference board. Do not silently replace the project's established art direction.

## Failure handling
If existing assets conflict, identify the canonical source of truth and ask for a decision before normalizing the whole pack.

## Acceptance criteria
A new asset can be judged against explicit rules and side-by-side examples; exceptions are traceable and style drift is detectable.
