# Minecraft Material and Surface Design

## Purpose
Make materials recognizable through form, color clusters, texture rhythm and lighting rather than random detail.

## Trigger
Use when authoring or critiquing material textures, props, armor, blocks, creatures or surface details.

## Inputs
Material identity, target resolution, style card, lighting direction, target runtime and reference.

## Workflow
1. Identify material properties: hard/soft, rough/smooth, opaque/translucent, organic/man-made, worn/new.
2. Define characteristic edge, highlight, shadow and texture-cluster behavior.
3. Separate large material regions before adding scratches, grain, seams or pores.
4. Keep texture detail scaled to the model and viewing distance.
5. Distinguish adjacent materials through value, hue, cluster direction and edge treatment.
6. Test under neutral and typical in-game lighting.
7. Remove decorative noise that does not reinforce material identity.

## Outputs
Material definition sheet, swatches, texture cluster rules, applied texture and review notes.

## Safety
Avoid copying distinctive commercial material packs. Maintain source/license records for external reference textures.

## Failure handling
If a material is not recognizable at native resolution, simplify and exaggerate its defining cues instead of adding more noise.

## Acceptance criteria
Each major material can be distinguished at intended viewing scale and remains consistent across the asset family.
