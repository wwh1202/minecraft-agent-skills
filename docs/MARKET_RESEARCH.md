# Minecraft Plugin Market Research Method

## Purpose

Find plugin opportunities suitable for an individual developer: meaningful demand, clear differentiation, feasible implementation, and sustainable maintenance cost.

## Source register

The following are research destinations, not claims that a current market audit has already been completed:

- Hangar — https://hangar.papermc.io/
- SpigotMC Resources — https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/
- Modrinth — https://modrinth.com/plugins
- CurseForge — https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/bukkit-plugins

Supplement with Paper documentation, GitHub repositories/issues/releases, plugin documentation, server-owner communities, and public discussion forums.

## Research procedure

1. Define the target segment: server type, version range, server size, operator skill, and budget.
2. Search each platform with equivalent keywords and categories.
3. Record comparable plugins, update dates, supported versions, pricing, license, feature claims, and public engagement metrics when visible.
4. Read recent reviews, issue reports, discussions, and changelogs to identify recurring unmet needs.
5. Inspect maintenance signals: release cadence, unresolved issues, compatibility lag, dependency health, and documentation quality.
6. Identify whether the gap is a missing product, poor UX, reliability issue, integration gap, or underserved niche.
7. Estimate an MVP boundary and ongoing support burden.
8. Score the opportunity and state uncertainty.
9. Re-check the evidence before making a build/no-build recommendation.

## Opportunity score

Rate each dimension 1–5 and explain the evidence:

- Pain severity (20%)
- Evidence of demand (20%)
- Competitive gap / differentiation (15%)
- Individual-developer feasibility (15%)
- Maintenance burden (15%, reverse-scored: lower burden is better)
- Monetization / distribution plausibility (10%)
- Platform and technical risk (5%, reverse-scored)

Weighted score = sum(weight × normalized rating). Do not present a score without the underlying observations and confidence level.

## Evidence hygiene

- Record access date and source URL.
- Capture the sample size and time window.
- Separate visible platform metrics from estimates.
- Do not treat downloads, stars, or likes as revenue or active installations.
- Avoid scraping where platform terms, robots rules, or access controls prohibit it.
- Respect rate limits and stop on CAPTCHA or access denial.
- Never fabricate reviews, sales, downloads, competitor features, or demand.
- Publicly report uncertainty and source coverage.

## Output template

A market report should include:
1. Executive recommendation: build / validate further / reject.
2. Target buyer and painful job.
3. Evidence table with source/date/observation/confidence.
4. Competitor comparison.
5. Gap hypothesis.
6. MVP feature boundary and explicit non-goals.
7. Engineering estimate and risk register.
8. Maintenance and support estimate.
9. Distribution and pricing hypothesis.
10. Next validation experiment.
